With Metwiegaaterkoffiehalen.nl, your team can quickly and fairly decide who’s on coffee duty! The frontend is built entirely with React, while the backend runs on a NodeJS application supported by microservices hosted on AWS.

The mobile app is developed in Flutter and available for both iOS and Android.
